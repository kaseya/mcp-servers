# Code of Conduct
Community Standards & Enforcement Policy

### 1. Our Pledge

The Kaseya Insights Community (the "**Community**") is Kaseya's community for practitioners using Kaseya's AI capabilities. It includes the kaseya/mcp-servers repository, its issues and discussions, and the related Discord and Teams channels. The Community is a forum. It is not a Kaseya product or service. Kaseya products and services, including Kaseya Insights and the Kaseya MCP servers, are governed by your Kaseya agreement and their own terms, not by the Community documents.

The Kaseya Insights Community is a community of practitioners that gather to help each other close the gap between tools and outcomes. The goal is a community where Participants can get help, share hard-won knowledge, and move faster than they could alone. All are invited to the community, provided that they contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.   

In general, if you are useful, honest and respectful – you are welcome.

---

### 2. Our Standards

#### Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include: 
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others in the community; 
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences, as well as the fact that people work in different environments, with different tools under different constraints; 
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; 
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes and learning from the experience; 
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community;
- Sharing knowledge generously, including when you might have gotten things wrong; and
- Keeping discussions focused on operational outcomes and technical substance. 

#### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of behavior that will not be tolerated include: 
- Trolling, insulting comments, discriminatory language and personal or political attacks;
- Public or private harassment;
- Publishing another person’s private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without their explicit permission;
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- Sharing another organization’s confidential information, such as credentials or internal systems configuration;
- Posting customer data, API keys or other credentials, or vulnerability details, in any Community channel (report vulnerabilities privately as described in the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md));
- Spamming the community, such as with vendor pitches or self-promotion unconnected to useful contribution;
- Deliberately misleading the community, such as about whether something works or the extent of testing conducted; and
- Other conduct which reasonably would be considered unprofessional, damaging or potentially damaging to another individual or entity.

---

### 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community. Examples of representing the Kaseya Insights Community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event. 

--- 
### 4. Enforcement

Kaseya is responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any act that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful to the community.   

Kaseya may act unilaterally or by consensus to enforce this Code of Conduct pursuant to procedures determined by Kaseya.  Such enforcement includes taking any of the following actions in good faith:  

- Removing content or members who violate this Code of Conduct, or where it is reasonable to believe they likely will violate this Code of Conduct;
- Editing or rejecting comments, commitments, code and other Contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct or as otherwise reasonably believed to be best for the community. 

Enforcement decisions are at the discretion of Kaseya. 

See [Governance Policy](GOVERNANCE.md) for more information about Kaseya’s decision-making procedures. 

---

### 5. Reporting Breached of this Code of Conduct

Should you wish to report an issue under this Code of Conduct, contact a Community moderator, or Kaseya through the support channels listed in the [README](../README.md).  

Reasonable efforts will be used to review and investigate reports promptly and fairly, and in consideration of privacy and confidentiality.  However, there is no promise that confidentiality and anonymity can be maintained throughout an investigation and enforcement action.   

Knowingly making false claims is also a violation of this Code of Conduct.
