# Security Policy
This repository is part of the Kaseya Insights Community. The Kaseya Insights Community (the "Community") is Kaseya's community for practitioners using Kaseya's AI capabilities. It includes the kaseya/mcp-servers repository, its issues and discussions, and the related Discord and Teams channels. The Community is a forum. It is not a Kaseya product or service. Kaseya products and services, including Kaseya Insights and the Kaseya MCP servers, are governed by your Kaseya agreement and their own terms, not by the Community documents.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Report security vulnerabilities in this repository or in the Kaseya-hosted MCP servers privately by email to disclosures@kaseya.com.
The Kaseya Vulnerability Disclosure Policy governs scope, safe harbor and any recognition. Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests, discussions, Discord or Teams, and do not include customer data or credentials in a report.

## If your account may be compromised
If you think your credentials or a Kaseya MCP server you use have been compromised, revoke the affected API credentials in the product immediately and contact Kaseya support. Your obligations to notify Kaseya are set out in your Kaseya agreement.
